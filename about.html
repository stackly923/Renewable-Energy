Quality standards

Define quality before delivery.

  • ✓
    Clear specifications

    Write measurable design and equipment requirements.

  • ✓
    Documented inspections

    Use installation checks and commissioning records to verify work.

  • ✓
    Practical handover

    Provide system documentation, maintenance guidance and escalation routes.

Electrical isolation, access, working at height and battery hazards require project-specific procedures and qualified teams.

Safety responsibilities belong in design reviews, installation planning, commissioning and ongoing operations.
