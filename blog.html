Technology explained

Look inside an
energy storage plan.

A battery proposal should explain the problem it solves. Backup, load shifting and peak reduction require different assumptions about operating hours, power and energy.

  • Separate power from energy

    Power describes delivery rate; energy describes how much can be stored.

  • Understand the schedule

    Review when charging happens and which loads need support.
