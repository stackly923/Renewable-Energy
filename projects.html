Impact, explained

Every number needs context.

Generation is an estimate

Sample annual generation depends on stated capacity and assumed resource conditions. It is not measured operating output.

Carbon estimates have a boundary

Solar, wind and hybrid examples use an illustrative factor of 0.71 tCO₂e/MWh, not a current official emissions factor.

Storage shifts energy

Battery storage does not generate electricity. Its carbon effect depends on charging source, dispatch and efficiency.
