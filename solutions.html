Frequently asked questions

A clearer starting point.

Where should our energy project begin?

Start with your location, twelve months of electricity bills, an outline of operations and available rooftop or land area. An assessment can then compare demand, generation potential and connection constraints.

Can solar and wind work together?

Yes. Their production profiles can complement each other, depending on the site. A hybrid assessment considers hourly generation and demand, grid access and whether storage adds useful flexibility.

Does a battery replace a grid connection?

Not automatically. Backup duration depends on usable energy, the connected load and recharge opportunities. Critical loads, power quality and system protection need a dedicated design.

How are savings and carbon benefits estimated?

Savings depend on production, self-consumption, tariffs, losses and operating costs. Carbon estimates require a stated emissions factor and reporting boundary. Our on-page calculator is illustrative and does not replace a site-specific study.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?

This website is a demonstration. The form validates your entries and clears after a successful demo submission; it does not send an enquiry to a server. No real project request is created.
